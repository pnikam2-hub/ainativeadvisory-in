Buyer brief · one page

What a district actually thinks — with the evidence attached.

JanPulse interviews residents in their own language, maps where opinion groups agree and where they split, and writes a brief in which every factual sentence is tied to the respondent evidence behind it and machine-checked before delivery. It is built for policy buyers who have to defend a finding in public, not just circulate a report.

What you receive

  • A map, not an average. Opinion groups, the statements that bridge all of them, and the statements that divide them.
  • Confidence on every figure. Agreement rates carry a credible interval, sample size and the group-level breakdown — no bare percentages.
  • A cited brief. Every factual clause carries one to five evidence references, and a checker recomputes every number.
  • A coverage report first. What your question set already has an answer to, how old that evidence is, and what genuinely needs fieldwork.
  • A reproducibility record. Snapshot, run, codebook and instrument versions, so a reviewer or auditor can re-derive the result.
  • A consent register. Who agreed to what, per respondent, including whether their words may be quoted or reused.

What we do not claim

  • Not a random sample. Respondents are recruited through partner communities and opt in. Every report names the sampling frame and where it may not generalise.
  • No map below the floor. Under roughly 30 respondents per group we report that no stable group structure exists instead of drawing one. Small groups are suppressed, not merged away.
  • No invented figures. Numbers in a brief are copied from evidence units and recomputed by code. Where a scorer has no measured accuracy, claims resting on it are labelled thin.
  • No synthetic people. Synthetic data is used only to demonstrate interfaces, and always labelled as such.
  • Consent, then erasure. Consent is captured per respondent — quoting and reuse included — re-checked at the point of use, and erasure is honoured with a receipt. Respondents are pseudonymous and their answers are held apart from contact details.
